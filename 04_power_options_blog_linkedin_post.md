Traders holding call options on power made fortunes—their $8/MWh premiums turned into $1,400/MWh payoffs overnight. Meanwhile, generators locked into fixed-price forwards faced catastrophic losses.

This tutorial walks through power options analytics: Black-Scholes pricing for power markets, swing option valuation with dynamic programming, volatility smile analysis, portfolio Greeks management, and real-time trading signal generation. The code demonstrates how to monetize volatility and create asymmetric payoffs.

This matters because power markets exhibit extreme volatility—prices routinely swing 50-200% within hours. Options provide asymmetric payoffs that let traders participate in favorable moves while limiting losses. In markets where prices can spike 500% instantly, options separate professionals from amateurs.
